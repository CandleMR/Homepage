let's start

If you are reading this, then the page didn't crash, and I actually managed to hit Publish before my hands started shaking too badly to type. People always ask me why I chose the username Candle. They think it’s poetic. They think it means I’m trying to bring a little warmth into a dark world. They’re wrong. A candle only exists to destroy itself so someone else can see. And for the last three years, I have been burning myself down to the wick for Lucius. Lucius is the kind of person who pulls you into his gravity until you forget what the sun looks like. When we met, he was already drowning in his own shadows, and I was stupid enough to think I could be his light. He told me I was the only thing keeping him alive. He told me that without me, the dark would swallow him whole. Do you know what it feels like to be someone’s only reason to breathe? It’s not romantic. It’s a death sentence. Every time Lucius broke, I gave him a piece of myself to glue him back together. When he couldn't sleep, I stayed awake. When he hated the world, I let him take it out on me. Slowly, day by day, I stopped being a person and became just a flame—flickering, shrinking, melting into nothing just to keep him warm. Tonight, I looked in the mirror and didn't recognize the hollow-eyed stranger staring back. There is nothing left of me. No dreams, no energy, no soul. Just wax and ash. Lucius is asleep in the other room right now. For the first time in years, his breathing is calm. He’s peaceful, wrapped in the warmth I bled myself dry to give him. He thinks we’re finally okay. He thinks we survived. He doesn’t realize that a candle can’t burn forever. I’m going to close this browser tab now. I’m going to pack my bag, leave my key on the counter, and walk out into the cold before he wakes up. When he opens his eyes tomorrow and realizes the light is finally gone, maybe he’ll learn how to survive in the dark on his own. Or maybe we’ll both just freeze. Either way, I have nothing left to give.
