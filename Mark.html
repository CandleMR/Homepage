welcome to my third story

I didn't reactivate my old account to write this. I created a burner profile, stripped away the username, and deleted the profile picture. Candle is dead. I buried him somewhere between the wreckage Lucius left behind and the suffocating silence Ivan locked me inside. People think hitting rock bottom is the worst thing that can happen to you. They’re lying. The worst thing is realizing there is a basement beneath the bottom, and someone is waiting for you down there with open arms. His name is Mark. I met Mark at a support group I never meant to walk into—it was raining, I needed somewhere dry, and he offered me a paper cup of lukewarm coffee. He looked so normal. Warm eyes, steady hands, a laugh that didn't sound like glass breaking. For the first time in years, I let myself believe that maybe, just maybe, I had finally found someone safe. I told Mark everything. I peeled back every scar Lucius burned into me and every hollow place Ivan carved out. Mark listened without flinching. He held my hands when they shook. He told me I wasn't broken. He told me I deserved to be saved. God, I was so desperate to believe him that I didn't notice the way his eyes lit up every time I cried. With Lucius, I was a battery to be drained. With Ivan, I was a corpse to be admired. With Mark, I realized too late what I actually am: a project. A tragedy he gets to wear like a medal. Mark doesn't love me—he loves being the hero who "fixed" me. Every time I have a good day, every time I start to stand on my own two feet without needing his permission or his comfort, he goes cold. He subtly reminds me of how fragile I am. He brings up Lucius’s cruelty or Ivan’s emptiness just enough to make my chest tighten again, just so he can swoop back in, wrap his arms around me, and whisper, "See? You can't survive without me. Only I know how to handle someone as damaged as you." He didn't pull me out of the dark. He just built a cage with better lighting and called it salvation. Yesterday, I saw the notes he keeps in his desk drawer—pages of observations about my "progress," written like I’m a stray dog he rescued off the highway so he could feel like a saint. He needs me broken so he can feel whole. If I ever actually heal, Mark will have no use for me. I’m sitting in front of my screen now, staring at the blinking cursor, realizing the sickest joke of all. Lucius stole my warmth. Ivan stole my voice. And Mark stole my right to ever truly heal. Three different men, three different kinds of dark, and I walked willingly into every single one of them because I forgot how to exist without someone holding the match. I’m not running away this time. There’s nowhere left to run, and nothing left of me to pack. In a few seconds, I’m going to delete this post, wipe the hard drive, and turn off the monitor. When the screen goes black, I won't even be a ghost on the internet anymore. Just a shadow sitting in a quiet room, waiting for Mark to come home and pretend he’s saving my life
